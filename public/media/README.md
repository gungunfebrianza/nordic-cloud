# Media

Video is never in the critical path. Every film has an AVIF poster, and the layout reserves space from the poster's
aspect ratio, so the page renders and scores the same with or without the videos present.

The posters in this folder are **neutral placeholders** generated by `scripts/make-posters.mjs`.
Replace each with a real frame from its film (same file name, same aspect ratio) when the films arrive.

## Specs

- **No audio track** in the vertical loops. The brand film may carry audio but never autoplays with sound;
  it starts muted, or on a click.
- **Loops** last a whole number of heartbeats (multiples of 2.4 s, e.g. 9.6 s or 14.4 s) and are seamless,
  so they tick in time with the page's motion.
- **Size targets:** vertical loops ≤ 1.5 MB each (webm), brand film ≤ 8 MB at 1080p.
  Encode at 24 or 30 fps; there is no need for 60.
- **Loading:** videos are lazy-loaded (`preload="none"`, mounted when near the viewport) and never load
  before first paint. With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, the poster is shown instead of the loop.
- **Visual rules (from the brief):** light, daylight surfaces; abstract systems (nodes, links, packets, dials,
  ledgers). No humanoid robots, glowing brains, circuit-board clichés, stock people at screens, or real company
  or coin logos.
- **Captions:** the brand film ships with `brand-film.en.vtt` before launch.
- **Brand film in #how:** the page checks for the film at build time. Until at least one of
  `brand-film.webm` / `brand-film.mp4` **and** `brand-film.en.vtt` are here, the frame shows the poster with
  "Film coming soon". Add the files and rebuild; the click-to-play player appears with no code change.
